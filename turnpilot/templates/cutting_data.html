Cutting data from catalogues

The planner takes ap, f and Vc from the confirmed rows: an insert's or a drill's ap / f by its code, a grade's Vc by the grade, the application (turning, grooving, parting, threading, drilling) and the material's catalogue group (Machine page, Materials). Every number keeps its catalogue and page, and the operator confirms it. A tool without confirmed rows for the job's material uses its own values, and its operations ask you to check them.
