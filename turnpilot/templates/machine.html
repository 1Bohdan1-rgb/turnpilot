Nothing here is guessed: an empty field is not known. When threading the feed is the pitch, so the Z axis moves at n·P mm/min; with a threading limit, thread operations keep within it, without one they ask you to check n·P. The spindle delivers power × efficiency; without the efficiency every roughing operation asks you to check the power. A value you change here is recorded as entered by the operator; values read from the passport show its page.

Bar stock

A part whose largest section is a hex of one of these sizes gets a hex bar as the suggested blank: its flats are not machined. Round bar sizes are set in the configuration.

Specific cutting force kc = kc1 · hm−mc (hm = feed for a 90° entering angle), as the tool maker's catalogue gives it for the material group. The cutting power of roughing is Pc = Vc · ap · f · kc / 60000 kW. A material without kc1 and mc is not checked. The seed values are placeholders: replace them with your catalogue's. The catalogue group (e.g. Sandvik Coromant P1.2) picks the confirmed rows of the cutting data for this material; without one, every operation uses the tool's own values and asks you to check them.
