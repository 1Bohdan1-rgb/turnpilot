Machine profile

When threading the feed is the pitch, so the Z axis moves at n·P mm/min. With a limit here the speed of thread operations is reduced to stay within it; without one every thread operation asks you to check n·P for this machine.

The spindle delivers power × efficiency (see the machine's documentation; often 0.75–0.85). Roughing passes are made thinner when the cutting power would exceed it; without it every roughing operation asks you to check the power.

Bar stock

A part whose largest section is a hex of one of these sizes gets a hex bar as the suggested blank: its flats are not machined. Round bar sizes are set in the configuration.
